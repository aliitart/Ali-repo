About us

We are a K-POP Dance Cover Crew that was founded in May 2022. We are a passionate group with a team mostly composed of self-taught dancers who enjoys K-POP and Dancing collectively and we hope to meet new people with similar vibes!

TEMPO's mindset is to work hard, seek growth and consistently learn to improve ourselves as dancers, with one of our goals being able to cohesively reach an intermediate dance level!

The group also strives to be involved in Winnipeg's local dance community by performing locally, and hosting events such as Random Play Dance!
